## Collaboration Approach
The team followed a practical sprint-style workflow adapted to academic deadlines and client communication. Client discussions, weekly milestones, and internal review sessions were used to progressively refine the project. Although some members had stronger technical responsibility than others, the team structure aimed to maintain a fair distribution of work by assigning members to development, testing, and documentation roles according to current project needs. Communication and decisions were supported through team discussions, project files, meeting records, and shared documentation.

## Evidence of Group Interaction Quality
The team maintained regular communication throughout the project and used those interactions to review requirements, discuss alternatives, and resolve issues. Technical decisions such as consultation workflow design, payment handling in Stripe test mode, dashboard improvements, admin visibility, and testing structure were not made in isolation; instead, they were informed by client expectations, internal feedback, and repeated team review. The development lead coordinated many implementation decisions, but testing and documentation members contributed by identifying practical issues, reviewing outputs, and improving the final quality of deliverables.

## Evidence of Role Effectiveness
Roles were assigned based on strengths and became more clearly defined as the project progressed:
- Sandeep contributed primarily through technical development, system integration, debugging, deployment readiness review, and cross-team coordination.
- Harshit and Rabin contributed through testing support, feature validation, and practical review of user experience and output correctness.
- Ahmed and Dipesh contributed through documentation support, user manual drafting, report alignment, and written evidence preparation.

This division allowed the team to balance development workload with testing and reporting needs in the final weeks of the project.

## Challenges and Adjustments
Several challenges affected the coordination process:
- Some content and branding updates depended on client responses and therefore could not always be finalised immediately.
- Live Stripe activation depended on access to the client's production account, so the team used Stripe developer test mode during implementation and testing.
- Consultation workflow and admin data visibility required several refinement cycles as the system moved closer to handover quality.

The team responded to these issues by continuing progress on all non-client-dependent work, maintaining test-mode functionality, documenting remaining dependencies clearly, and preparing the system so live activation could happen later without major redesign.
